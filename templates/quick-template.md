---

## Formatting Rules

1. Keep each method to 3 lines max (why, time/evidence, what you'll learn)
2. Only 1 companion tool (not 3 like the full assessment)
3. No AI acceleration section
4. No evidence convergence map
5. Always include karaoke check — even in quick mode
6. Always include attribution
